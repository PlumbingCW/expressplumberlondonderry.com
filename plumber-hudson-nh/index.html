Sewer and drain emergencies in Hudson

A backup that reaches the lowest drain in the house is the most common reason a Hudson caller dials after midnight. We start with a cable to restore flow, then run a camera down the line to see why it stopped. Our sewer line repair page explains the options when the camera shows a cracked or collapsed section.

If roots are the cause, a main that is only cabled will clog again within weeks. Sewer line cleaning with a cutting head or jetting clears the pipe wall, and a follow-up sewer camera inspection shows the result.

Keep one rule in mind while you wait. Do not run the washer, dishwasher or showers, because every gallon goes to the lowest open drain.

Water heaters: old tanks and newer ones fail differently

An older tank and a newer one fail in different ways. A rusted tank leaks at the base and cannot be repaired. A tank with a failed element or thermostat can be, and we diagnose which you have before suggesting a swap.

When the tank has failed, water heater replacement is the honest answer, and we can often schedule it for the same day when stock and schedule allow. If you are replacing before it fails, water heater installation gives you time to compare tank and tankless options.

We quote the price before work starts, and we tell you if a repair is not worth the money.

Before we arrive in Hudson: burst pipe or active leak

Do these in order. Each one takes under a minute.

Find and close the main valve

Look near the meter or where the supply line enters the basement. Turn it fully closed, then open a low faucet to drain the pipes.

Cut power near the water

If water is dripping near a panel, outlet or water heater, turn off those breakers only from dry ground. If you cannot reach the panel safely, say so on the call.

Move what you can

Lift rugs and valuables, and place buckets under drips. Do not tackle a ceiling bulging with water. Leave it for the plumber.

Dial (603) 815-9179 and tell us if sewage is involved. If the backup is in the street, also call Hudson Public Works or the police dispatch number above.
