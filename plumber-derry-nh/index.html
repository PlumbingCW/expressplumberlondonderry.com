Before we arrive in Derry: burst pipe or active leak

These four steps limit damage while the plumber is on the way.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. It is usually where the line enters the basement or near the meter. If it will not turn, tell the dispatcher.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, a panel or a water heater, switch off those breakers from a dry spot. Do not wade through standing water to reach the panel.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water. Open drains in a flooded basement should stay clear, and children and pets should stay out.

Call the 24/7 line

Dial (603) 815-9179 and say what is leaking, where the main valve is, and whether you smell sewage. We tell you who we can send and when.
