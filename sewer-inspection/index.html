Sewer Camera Inspection in Londonderry, NH

A waterproof camera on a flexible cable shows what is wrong inside the pipe, so nothing is guessed.

Call (603) 815-9179

A sewer camera inspection is a video survey of the pipe that carries wastewater out of your house. A technician feeds a small camera head through a cleanout or a removed toilet and watches the pipe on a monitor, noting its material, slope, joints and any damage along the way.

Most inspections are planned ahead. Buyers, owners with repeat clogs and homeowners planning a landscaping or driveway project all book one. If sewage is backing up now, call the 24/7 line and ask for a plumber to clear it first.

Who books a sewer inspection and why

Home buyers

A standard home inspection does not look inside the buried sewer line. A camera pass before closing shows roots, offsets or a collapsed section while the price can still be discussed.

Owners with repeat clogs

If a cable clears the line and it slows again, the camera shows the cause, whether that is roots, grease, scale or a sag.

Anyone planning a dig or a build

Before a driveway, deck or addition goes over the sewer line, the camera records its depth and path.

Owners of older lines

Cast iron, clay and fiber conduit deteriorate with age. A survey tells you how much life the pipe has left.

What the camera looks for

The camera travels from the cleanout toward the street or the septic tank. We stop at anything unusual and mark its distance from the entry point.

  • Root intrusion at joints, and how dense the mat is.
  • Cracks, breaks, offset joints and sections that have collapsed.
  • Bellies or sags where water and solids collect.
  • Grease buildup, scale and mineral deposits.
  • Pipe material changes, such as cast iron meeting plastic.
  • Connections that should not be there, like a roof drain tied into the sewer.

A locator on the camera head lets us find the pipe from above ground. That tells you exactly where it runs and how deep, and it shows where to dig if a repair is needed.

What you get after the inspection

You watch the video with us on site or see the findings the same visit. We explain what is minor, what is worth watching and what needs repair. Our approach is to describe the pipe honestly, not to find work.

If the line is sound, we say so and you have a baseline for future comparison. If there is damage, we lay out the options in plain terms, from cleaning to lining to replacement, and we give you a clear price before work starts. The repair options are on our sewer line repair page.

Town sewer or septic: tell us which you have

Londonderry homes connect either to town sewer or to a septic tank. The inspection works for both, but the route differs. On town sewer we follow the line to the property line or the main. On septic we follow it to the tank inlet.

Ask the Londonderry Department of Public Works at 603-432-1100, extension 460, whether your address is on a sewer main. If you are buying, that answer belongs in your due diligence. A septic system also needs its own inspection by a septic professional, which is separate from a house line camera pass.

If the camera finds damage that will need a dig, Building and Zoning Enforcement at 603-432-1100, extension 440, can say what the town requires.

What a sewer inspection costs

We give you a clear price before work starts. These factors shape it:

  • Whether a cleanout is accessible or a toilet must be pulled.
  • The length of the line and any branches.
  • Whether the line needs cleaning first so the camera can see.
  • Whether you want a locating survey to mark the path.
  • A written summary or recorded video for a buyer or lender.

Homeowners insurance does not usually pay for an inspection, since it is a maintenance cost and not a claim. Call (603) 815-9179 to book a visit and ask about same-day appointments when available.
