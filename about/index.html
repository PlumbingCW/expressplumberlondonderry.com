About Londonderry Express Plumbing

We answer the phone at any hour and send a licensed plumber to your door.

Call (603) 815-9179

Londonderry Express Plumbing is a 24/7 plumbing dispatch service based at 75 Gilcreast Road in Londonderry, New Hampshire. When you call, a dispatcher takes the address and symptoms and schedules a licensed plumber for Londonderry or a nearby town.

We built the service around the calls homeowners make most often: backed-up drains, hidden leaks, water heater failures and burst pipes. Those are the problems that cannot wait, so the line is staffed at night, on weekends and on holidays.

How we work

  1. You callTell the dispatcher what is wrong and where. They tell you what to shut off while you wait.
  2. We scheduleA licensed plumber is scheduled for your address, with same-day appointments when available.
  3. You see the priceThe plumber diagnoses the problem and gives you a clear price before work starts.
  4. The repair is explainedYou learn what failed, what was done and what to watch for.

What you can count on

  • Licensed and insured plumbers on every job.
  • A clear price before work starts.
  • Same-day appointments when available.
  • A 24/7 line that is answered by a person.

Ask for the plumber's license on any job. New Hampshire licenses master and journeyman plumbers through its plumbers licensing board, which you can reach at 603-271-3267. We would rather you check than take our word for it.

The problems homeowners call about most

Most calls start with a symptom and not a service name. People describe a meter that will not stop running, a tub that gurgles when the washer drains, a puddle under the water heater or a ceiling stain after a cold night. We route each call by the symptom, so you do not need to know the technical term.

The services we list reflect those calls: drain cleaning, leak detection, water heater repair, burst pipe repair and toilet repair come up the most. Planned work such as repiping and installations follows once the urgent problem is solved.

What a first visit looks like

The plumber confirms the cause before touching anything, explains it in plain terms and gives you a clear price before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead. When the job is done, the plumber walks you through what changed and what to watch for in the following days, so the repair does not feel like a mystery.

What we do not do

We do not promise an exact arrival time, because traffic, weather and the length of the previous job all change it. We do not quote a final price over the phone for a problem nobody has seen. We tell you the process and the price factors, and the plumber gives a clear price after diagnosing the problem.

We also do not treat gas smells as plumbing calls. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call anyone else.

Londonderry, the town we dispatch from

Londonderry is a Rockingham County town of 25,826 people at the 2020 census, with villages at Londonderry, North Londonderry and Wilson. NH 128 (Mammoth Road) runs north to south, and I-93 Exits 4 and 5 connect the town to NH 102 and NH 28. Knowing those roads helps us plan an arrival when a storm slows traffic.

Water in town comes from more than one source. Many homes use private wells, and the town has been planning public water extensions in areas affected by PFAS contamination. We tell customers to test well water and to ask the state environmental agency, NHDES, about testing and treatment programs.

Local offices matter when a job needs a permit. The Londonderry Building and Zoning Enforcement office at 268B Mammoth Road, 603-432-1100, extension 440, handles plumbing permits, and the Department of Public Works answers sewer questions at extension 460.

How to check any plumber before you hire

  • Ask for the plumber's license and the name on it.
  • Confirm it with the New Hampshire plumbers licensing board at 603-271-3267.
  • Ask for the price in plain terms before work begins.
  • Ask what is being repaired or replaced and why.
  • Keep the invoice, since your insurer or the town may ask for it.

The board licenses master and journeyman plumbers under RSA 329-A. A master plumber may employ others, and a journeyman works under a master's direction. The same checks apply to us as to anyone else.
