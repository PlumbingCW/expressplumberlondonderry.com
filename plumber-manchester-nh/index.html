The city water system and your plumbing

Manchester Water Works is the city's water utility. We do not quote a hardness number for Manchester, because we have not verified one. If hard water bothers you, read the utility's annual Water Quality Report and test your tap.

That report is also the right place to look if water looks cloudy or tastes different. If the water is clear from the street but weak inside your building, the cause is probably on your side of the meter. That is where we start.

Drains, sewers and the lines under the street

Older cities have older sewers, and a backup that reaches a basement drain can come from a private lateral or from the street main. We camera the lateral to find out. If the problem is in the street main, it is not on your property, and we explain how to report it to the city.

For private blockages, drain cleaning clears the line, and hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall when grease or roots keep bringing the clog back. We always tell you before adding a second step.

Permits can come into play when a repair changes the pipe layout. Permit rules vary, so ask the building department for the city where the work is happening.

From Londonderry, the I-93 corridor leads into Manchester's south end. We also serve Litchfield and Derry from the same base, so a busy night in one town does not leave another without a plumber. Bedford calls come through the same line.

Manchester work falls under the International Plumbing Code (IPC) in the State Building Code, with the city's own permit process on top.

Before we arrive in Manchester: active leak steps

In a house, a flat or a storefront, these steps hold the damage down.

Close the main valve

In a house it is usually in the basement. In a multi-unit building, close the unit shutoff, and ask the building manager about the building main.

Switch off power to wet areas

Turn off breakers for any wet circuit from a dry spot. Never touch a panel while standing in water.

Keep people and stock clear

Move people and stored goods away from the water, and put out wet floor signs if the public is nearby.
