Contact Londonderry Express Plumbing

The fastest way to reach us is the phone, and the line is answered 24/7.

Call (603) 815-9179 any time. Tell the dispatcher your address, which fixture is affected and whether water or sewage is spreading. We do not use contact forms, because plumbing problems are better solved by talking.

Contact details

  • Phone, 24/7: (603) 815-9179
  • Address: 75 Gilcreast Rd, Londonderry, NH 03053

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and the town.
  • Which fixtures or rooms are affected.
  • Whether water is still escaping and if you found the main shutoff.
  • Whether you have a well, a town water connection, a sewer line or a septic tank.
