Emergency Water Heater Repair in Londonderry, NH

A water heater that floods a garage or leaves a house cold in January needs a plumber now, not next week.

Call (603) 815-9179

Water heater repair is urgent when the tank is leaking, when the burner will not light in cold weather, or when the house has no hot water at all. Our 24/7 line answers at night and on weekends, and we send a licensed plumber the same day when the schedule allows.

While you wait, close the cold water valve on top of the heater and switch off its power or gas control. If water is spreading, turn off the main water valve and keep the electric panel dry. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

No hot water: the usual causes

Callers describe this one in plain terms. The shower runs cold, the dishwasher never gets hot, or the water is warm for two minutes and then fades. Each symptom points to a different part.

What you noticeLikely causeTypical repair
No hot water at all, electric heaterFailed heating element or tripped high-limit switchTest and replace the element or reset the switch
No hot water, gas heaterPilot out, bad thermocouple or failed gas controlRelight, replace thermocouple or control valve
Hot water fades after a few minutesBroken dip tube or one dead elementReplace the dip tube or the lower element
Water is lukewarmThermostat set low, sediment on the elementsAdjust thermostat, flush the tank
Rumbling or popping noiseSediment hardened on the tank bottomFlush, or plan a replacement if the tank is old

We confirm the cause with a meter or a gas test before we replace anything. If the part is cheap and the tank is sound, we repair it. If the tank itself has failed, we say so.

A leaking water heater: what to do first

Leaks are the most common reason people call us about water heaters. The water may come from the tank, from a fitting on top, from the relief valve or from a nearby pipe that only looks like the heater.

  1. Shut off the supplyClose the cold water inlet valve above the heater. Turn off the electric breaker or set the gas control to off.
  2. Find the sourceDry the area and watch where the first drop forms. Water at a pipe joint or valve is often repairable.
  3. Protect the floorMove stored items, lay towels and use a wet vacuum on standing water. A garage slab or basement floor drain will carry the rest.
  4. Call the 24/7 lineTell the dispatcher the tank size, the fuel and where the water is coming from.

A tank that leaks from its body cannot be patched. The steel liner has corroded through, and a replacement is the safe answer. Our water heater replacement page explains that process, and a heater that failed at 2 a.m. is usually swapped the same day.

Pilot lights, thermocouples and burner problems

A gas heater that keeps going out usually has a worn thermocouple, a dirty pilot orifice or a draft problem in the vent. We clean, test and replace those parts, and we check that exhaust leaves the house properly.

If you smell gas or the heater smells of burnt dust and soot, stop and ventilate. Gas heaters produce carbon monoxide when poorly vented, so a working carbon monoxide alarm near the sleeping area matters.

Please do not relight a pilot that keeps failing. A repeat failure tells us something else is wrong, and a plumber should check it.

Electric, gas and heat pump models

Electric heaters fail at the elements and thermostats. Gas heaters fail at the pilot, burner and gas control. Heat pump water heaters add a compressor and a condensate drain, and their error codes point to specific faults.

We repair all three types when parts are available. We also tell you when a repair costs more than the heater is worth, which is common on tanks past 10 years old. If you are weighing a switch to a tankless water heater, we explain the trade-offs before you decide.

Tankless units and the flush they need

Tankless heaters fail differently. Error codes, bursts of cold water and reduced flow often trace to scale inside the heat exchanger. A flush and descale restores the flow in many cases, and homes on private wells may need it more often. Test your tap water and ask your water utility for its annual quality report to judge how hard your water is.

Why water heaters fail in Londonderry homes

New Hampshire winters push heaters hard. Incoming water is very cold, and a tank in an unheated garage or basement works longer to keep up. Pipes near an exterior wall can also freeze and split, and a leak at the heater may start there.

Many Londonderry homes use private wells, and well water carries minerals and sediment into the tank. The state environmental agency offers programs for well testing, and a water test also tells us whether a filter or a flush schedule makes sense. Sediment is the most common reason tanks fail early.

What water heater repair costs

A repair price comes before we touch the element, valve or burner. These factors set it:

  • The part that failed, such as an element, thermocouple, valve or gas control.
  • The fuel type and the model, since parts for some brands take longer to find.
  • Whether the tank can be saved or a replacement is the sounder choice.
  • Access, venting and code updates needed when the work is done.
  • After-hours, weekend or holiday dispatch.

Homeowners policies often cover sudden water damage from a failed tank but not the heater itself. Check with your agent, and photograph the damage before you clean up. For your own number, call (603) 815-9179.
