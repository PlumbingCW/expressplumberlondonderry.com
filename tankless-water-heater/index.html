Tankless Water Heater in Londonderry, NH

Install, repair and flush: three jobs that keep a tankless heater delivering steady hot water.

Call (603) 815-9179

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows, so there is no tank to leak or run out. Installation, repair and annual flushing keep it working. In Londonderry, NH, we install gas and electric units, and we service most major brands.

Tankless heaters need a larger gas line or a heavy electrical circuit, correct venting and a maintenance plan. We cover all three.

Is a tankless heater right for your house?

Good fit

Smaller homes with limited floor space, households that run out of tank hot water, and builds that already have a large gas line.

Needs planning

Houses with small gas lines or limited electrical capacity. The upgrade can change the project cost.

Think twice

Homes with heavy sediment in the well water, unless a filter and flush plan are in place.

Pair with

A recirculation line if you want hot water at far fixtures without a long wait.

We walk through it with you. If a tank is the better value, we say so. See water heater installation for tank and heat pump options.

Installing a tankless unit

  1. Size the unitWe match flow rate to the fixtures that run together, and the winter inlet temperature in New Hampshire.
  2. Check gas and powerWe verify gas line size or electrical capacity, and upgrade if needed.
  3. Vent correctlyGas units need sealed venting that suits the model.
  4. Connect and commissionWe install isolation valves for flushing, set temperature and test.

Isolation valves matter. They let us flush the heat exchanger later without cutting pipe.

Tankless flush and descale

Minerals and sediment collect inside the heat exchanger, and the unit works harder as they build up. The signs are error codes, a reduced flow, temperature swings and a louder burner. A flush with a descaling solution restores performance in many cases.

Plan a flush at least yearly, and more often on well water that carries sediment. Test your tap and ask your water utility for its quality report. A visit takes roughly an hour and often prevents a repair.

Repairing tankless heaters

ProblemLikely cause
Error code on the displayScale, flame sensor, vent or flow problem
Water goes cold mid-showerLow flow, scaled exchanger, inlet screen clogged
Unit leaksFailed fitting, condensate drain or heat exchanger
Unit will not igniteGas supply, igniter or vent obstruction

If a gas unit leaks gas or vent fumes, shut it off, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911. For a leaking tankless unit, shut the water and power and call us. If the heater is beyond repair, our replacement team can swap it.

Neither is right for every house. We size the choice to your fixtures, your fuel and your well or town water.

Gas or electric tankless

Gas units deliver high flow and need a vent and a gas line sized for the burner. Electric units are simpler to vent but can need heavy circuits that older panels cannot carry. A panel upgrade can change the total project cost, so we check the panel first.

Point-of-use electric minis suit a single sink or a distant bath. They do not replace a whole-house heater.

Winter and well water in Londonderry

Cold New Hampshire inlet water lowers the flow a tankless heater can deliver. Size the unit for the winter, not the summer. Private wells can add sediment, so test the water and consider a filter.
